# FractureBridge v0.4 — UI clarity pass

Focused on presentation quality and usability without adding new clinical claims or workflow scope.

- Reduced Worklist density and removed overlapping micro-labels.
- Replaced four KPI cards with a single calm queue summary bar.
- Simplified table columns to patient, finding, elapsed time, owner, status.
- Kept follow-up caveat once at the bottom instead of repeating it in each row.
- Simplified Overview into one message, one KPI strip, one 4-step workflow, and two supporting cards.
- Replaced the cramped five-box Safety pipeline with three spacious phases: Find & check, Review & own, Engage & close.
- Reduced repeated safety/architecture detail while preserving all critical guardrails.
- Simplified Analytics around three questions instead of dense metric grids.
- Moved demo role selection into the sidebar to eliminate an extra horizontal control bar.
- Increased primary content width and whitespace for executive-demo readability.
- Softened card styling and reduced monospace usage.
