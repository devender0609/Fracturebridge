# FractureBridge v0.6.2

## Analytics visual restoration

- Restored the full-width analytics layout from the earlier prototype that the project owner preferred.
- Category 1: case-finding KPI strip plus exclusion-reason bar chart.
- Category 2: operations KPI strip plus weekly found-versus-closed line chart.
- Category 3: care-process cascade plus illustrative pre/post follow-up chart.

- Reframed exclusion reasons as quality-improvement signals rather than labeling them simply as false positives.
