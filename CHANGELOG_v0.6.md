# FractureBridge v0.6 — best-of-both refinement

This version returns to the visual language of the v0.2 refined prototype while retaining the strongest later workflow and safety improvements.

## Design direction preserved
- v0.2 card-based clinical dashboard aesthetic
- navy/teal/violet/amber clinical palette
- compact sidebar and clear section hierarchy
- table-based worklist rather than card-only queue
- signature FractureBridge overview and case-detail visuals

## Improvements carried forward
- role-aware worklist context for care coordinator, clinician, and quality leader
- cleaner worklist with fewer columns and no overlapping tags
- safer wording: missing connected evidence is not treated as proof that care did not occur
- no invented performance percentages or pseudo-precise pilot metrics
- simpler Safety & Design page with a clear AI-to-human handoff
- preserved human approval for clinical action and patient communication
- safer patient-facing language for compression deformities
- clarified that integration is conceptual and no live EHR connection exists
- guided demo shortened to the core clinical story

## Clinical boundary
FractureBridge remains a workflow-support prototype. It does not diagnose osteoporosis, determine fracture mechanism autonomously, order tests, prescribe treatment, or send patient communication without human approval.
