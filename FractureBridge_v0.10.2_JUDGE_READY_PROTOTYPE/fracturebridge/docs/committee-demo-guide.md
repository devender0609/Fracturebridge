# Committee demo guide

## If you send the link and say nothing
The Overview opens with **New to FractureBridge? Start the 60-second guided demo.** Eight captioned steps that dim the
page and highlight what is being explained, ending on *Explore the prototype*. No instruction needed.

## If you are driving — 60 seconds

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Overview | "A vertebral compression fracture gets documented on a scan ordered for something else. Find, check, route." |
| 0:12 | Point at the 171 / 97 bar | "171 already had follow-up visible — the system stands down. 97 go to a person as a potential gap. Illustrative simulation, not our data." |
| 0:22 | **Open the case** | "Margaret, 74." Then stop talking for five seconds and let them read *Why this case is here*. |
| 0:35 | Point at *What the system already checked* | "Seven configured sources searched. No relevant bone-health follow-up found in those configured sources — a review signal, not proof that care did not occur elsewhere." |
| 0:45 | *Assign an owner*, then open *Document why this case should not continue* | "One primary action. Four ways a case can legitimately end, and only one of them reflects on the screen." |
| 0:55 | **Analytics** → *Where are open cases waiting?* | "Case finding is half of it. This shows where follow-through stalls." |
| 1:05 | **How it works** | "AI searches and summarizes; people decide. EHR integration is a concept, with nothing connected." |

## For a quality leader in the room
Click **View as → Quality leader** in the sidebar. It opens Analytics and makes the case view read-only.

## The three questions that always come
- *Is this real data?* No — fictional patients and simulated values, labelled at every chart. Analytics defaults to the
  measurement framework and shows no numbers until you switch to the illustrative scenario.
- *How is this different from an alert?* An alert dies when dismissed. A case here has an owner, a clock, and a required
  disposition with a recorded reason.
- *Do we not already have a fracture liaison service?* Yes, and this does not replace it. It is intended to help
  documented vertebral-fracture findings enter an accountable pathway they may not otherwise reach.
