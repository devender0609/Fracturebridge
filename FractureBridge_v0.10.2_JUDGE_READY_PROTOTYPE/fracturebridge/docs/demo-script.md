# Guided demo — under 75 seconds

Click **Guided demo** in the sidebar. Seven steps, roughly ten seconds each.

**1 — Overview.** "A fracture is already documented in the report. The follow-up evidence check separates the patients
who can stand down — 171 here — from the 97 that need a person. Those 97 are potential gaps, not confirmed ones."

**2 — Margaret Ellison.** "A CT for abdominal pain. The impression also documents an incidental chronic-appearing L1
compression deformity. 102 days have passed."

**3 — Follow-up evidence check.** "No relevant follow-up evidence is visible in the connected demonstration sources.
That is a review signal, not proof that care did not occur — it may have happened outside this record."

**4 — Human review.** "A person confirms the context and checks for outside care, then either assigns an owner or closes
the case with a documented reason. Three kinds of closure, and only one of them counts against the screen."

**5 — Engagement.** "The patient letter is AI-drafted and cannot send itself. A human edits and approves it, and the
audit trail records that this was a simulated outreach — nothing was sent."

**6 — Analytics.** "A pilot would measure case finding, operational burden, and process completion. Anything not
measured says *To measure*. Reduction in future fractures is not an initial-pilot claim."

**7 — Safety & design.** "AI searches and summarizes. People decide. Every closure carries a named person and a reason."

## Likely questions

- *How is this different from an EHR alert?* — An alert dies when it is dismissed. Here a case has an owner, a clock,
  and a required disposition, and the reason is recorded and counted.
- *Don't we already have a fracture liaison service?* — Yes, and this does not replace it. An FLS sees the patients who
  reach it, mostly hip and clinical fractures. The incidental vertebral finding on an abdominal CT often never arrives.
- *What is the accuracy?* — Unknown until a pilot. That is what the case-finding category measures, and the
  screening-exclusion reasons are the defect report.
- *Are these real numbers?* — No. The funnel and the charts are a labelled simulation; the operational metrics say
  *To measure*.
